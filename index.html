Curiosity
looks good on you.

01Is my home right for solar?+

Your roof’s orientation, usable space, condition and shading all affect its potential. Your energy use matters too. A property assessment is the starting point for a suitable design.

02Should I add a battery?+

A battery stores surplus solar electricity for later. Its value depends on your usage, goals and system costs. Not every home needs one; it’s a useful option to discuss.

03What about night-time or blackouts?+

Panels do not generate at night. You can use stored battery energy or electricity from the grid. Blackout backup needs compatible equipment and a suitable installation; a battery alone does not guarantee it.

04What could I save?+

That depends on your property, system, energy use and electricity tariff. A useful quote should explain its assumptions and estimated output. A recent power bill helps start that conversation.
